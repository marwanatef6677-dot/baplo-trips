---
name: Pablo profile photo privacy
description: User photo requirements and private visibility rules for customer avatars and captain vehicle photos.
---

Customers may add an optional profile photo. Captains must upload a photo showing themselves with their car or motorcycle before their account can be reviewed and accept rides. The owner can view it for review; ride participants can view the captain photo only after assignment. Customer avatars are not shared with other users by default.

**Why:** The user asked for customers to register with a name and photo and for captains to be photographed with their vehicle for credibility. Keeping those photos private limits unnecessary exposure of identity images.

**How to apply:** Keep user photos in private storage and gate each image endpoint by account ownership, owner review access, or an assigned ride relationship. Replacing a captain photo requires review again.
