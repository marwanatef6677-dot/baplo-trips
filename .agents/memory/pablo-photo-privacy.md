---
name: Pablo profile photo privacy
description: User photo requirements and visibility rules for customer avatars and captain vehicle photos.
---

Customers may add an optional profile photo. Captains must upload a photo showing themselves with their car or motorcycle before their account can be reviewed and accept rides. All signed-in Pablo users may view account photos; unauthenticated visitors may not. The owner reviews captain vehicle photos, and replacing one requires review again.

Captains must also upload the front and back images of both their driving license and vehicle license before requesting approval. Keep these documents in private storage available only to authorized administration, retain them for administrative re-review, and never expose them to customers or other captains. Replacing any document resets captain approval; no captain can see or accept ride requests until the owner approves the complete file.

**Why:** The user asked for customers to register with a name and photo, captains to be photographed with their vehicle for credibility, and then explicitly chose visibility to all signed-in Pablo users rather than the public internet. The user later requested private, reviewable copies of both sides of the captain's two licenses.

**How to apply:** Keep account images and captain documents in private storage. Account photos are limited to authenticated Pablo users; captain license documents are restricted to owner/admin endpoints. Do not make signed documents publicly accessible, and do not claim Egyptian legal compliance without lawyer review. Replacing captain photos or documents requires administrative review again.
