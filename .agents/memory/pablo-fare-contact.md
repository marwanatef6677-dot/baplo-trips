---
name: Pablo fare and contact rules
description: The agreed fare formula, adjustable ride offers, and customer-captain contact privacy.
---

Pablo's fare is EGP 20 to start, plus EGP 10 per kilometer and EGP 1 per minute, with an EGP 40 minimum. The customer sees an estimate before requesting and can adjust the offer in EGP 5 steps. Do not treat EGP 20–60 as a fixed cap; the owner said that range was only an example. Driver queue cards should show the final offer so each captain can accept or leave the request.

Customer-captain chat and phone contact become available after a captain accepts. Do not expose a customer's phone number in the public driver queue.

**Why:** The owner reaffirmed the original fare formula and requested customer-adjustable offers that captains choose to accept. Restricting contact to the assigned captain protects customer phone privacy.

**How to apply:** Keep the minimum fare at EGP 40, base commission on the accepted offer, and keep customer contact details hidden until assignment.
