name: Pablo customer feedback
description: Separate customer reviews of the captain and of the Pablo app.
---

After a completed ride, keep the customer's captain review (stars and an optional comment) separate from their star rating of Pablo and optional app feedback.

**Why:** The user asked for captain comments and ratings, plus a distinct rating of Pablo.

**How to apply:** Do not merge app feedback into the captain's review or show Pablo's customer feedback to the captain.
