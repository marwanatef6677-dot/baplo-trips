---
name: Aman parental transparency
description: Privacy boundary for the standalone parental-safety app.
---

For Aman, keep monitoring and location-sharing clear and visible to the child. Do not hide app-usage or location collection, read private messages, or expose account content.

**Why:** the user chose to continue with the transparent parental-safety version after asking about concealed monitoring.

**How to apply:** keep any future app-pairing, location sharing, and usage controls permission-based, visible, and limited to safety needs.
