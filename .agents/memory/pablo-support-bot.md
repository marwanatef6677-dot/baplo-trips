---
name: Pablo customer support model
description: Product decisions for guided help, AI answers, and owner-reviewed support tickets.
---

Pablo customer support combines guided answers for confirmed app facts with AI answers to open questions. If the customer cannot resolve an issue, they can create a persistent ticket for owner follow-up. Owners can send a customer-visible reply on the ticket; internal owner notes stay private. Customers can also reach support by phone or WhatsApp.

**Why:** The user chose both guided and AI support and asked for in-app help when something is missing or a problem occurs, with staff replies and phone/WhatsApp as additional channels.

**How to apply:** Preserve the owner-reviewed ticket path and keep public staff replies separate from internal notes. Use app-verified information for AI answers; do not invent ride status, refunds, or support response times.
