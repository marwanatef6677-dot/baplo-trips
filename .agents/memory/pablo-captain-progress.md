---
name: Pablo captain levels and feedback
description: Captain progression rewards and customer feedback expectations for Pablo.
---

Captain level starts at 0 and advances by one for every 10 completed rides, up to Level 10. Each earned level gets an in-app badge, not a cash reward. Customer feedback includes a quick in-ride comfort/help check and a post-ride review of the captain, driving safety, vehicle cleanliness, and comfort, with an optional note. Car rides also ask about air conditioning; motorcycle rides do not.

**Why:** The owner requested ride-based levels and selected an in-app badge or feature as the reward, without specifying monetary value. Motorcycle passenger rides were later added, so car-only feedback such as air conditioning must not be required for a motorcycle.

**How to apply:** Count completed rides only; do not award wallet credit unless the owner explicitly changes the reward policy. Require air-conditioning feedback only for car rides.
