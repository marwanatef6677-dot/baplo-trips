---
name: Pablo weekly discount wheel
description: Customer weekly prize spins, promo-code redemption, and captain compensation.
---

Customers may spin once every rolling seven days. A winning spin issues a personal promo code that the customer enters manually when booking a passenger ride; it is not applied automatically. The code is limited to one completed passenger ride and expires after seven days. A canceled ride does not consume the code while it remains valid.

The wheel applies to passenger rides only, not product delivery. Pablo funds the discount. The captain collects the discounted cash amount, then receives an equal credit in the captain wallet after the ride is completed. Wallet credits offset future Pablo commissions and are not cash withdrawals.

**Why:** The owner chose weekly customer codes, passenger rides only, Pablo-funded discounts, and captain compensation through the existing wallet.

**How to apply:** Preserve the manual code-entry step, per-customer limits, completion-based settlement, and the distinction between wallet credit and cash when changing promotions or fare settlement.
